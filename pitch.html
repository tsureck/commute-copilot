02 — The Solution

Commute Copilot

An AI agent that monitors your commute route in real time and tells you what to do — not just what's happening. Voice-first, proactive, personal.

Proactive, not reactive

Checks train status, weather, and your calendar every 5 minutes between 6–10 AM. Sends a push notification before you leave home: "RE4 cancelled. Next reliable option at 08:34. Work from home until then."

Voice-first conversation

Ask follow-up questions by voice: "What if I need to be there by 11?" The AI reasons over stored context (train data, weather, calendar) and responds with a spoken recommendation.

Actionable decisions, not data

Outputs one of 4 clear actions: Leave now, Leave earlier, Wait and leave later, or Work from home. Each with a confidence score and reasoning you can drill into.

04 — Go-to-Market

First 10 customers,
then 10,000

Start hyper-local on the most disrupted corridor. Expand route by route as the product proves itself.

1

Hamburg ↔ Bremen corridor

~391,900 cross-border commuters into Hamburg alone. The RE4 line is notoriously unreliable. Target commuters on this specific route through local Reddit (r/hamburg, r/bremen), Jodel, and LinkedIn posts. Goal: 50 beta users in week 1.

2

Deutschlandticket communities

14.6M subscribers actively discussing transit pain on social media. Partner with Deutschlandticket influencers and transit advocacy groups. Freemium converts frustrated users who already pay €58/mo for unreliable service.

3

B2B through HR / People Ops

Pitch to hybrid companies in Hamburg & Bremen: "Know which of your employees can actually make it in today." Target 3–5 mid-size companies (100–500 employees) with flexible work policies. Offer 30-day free pilot.

4

Expand to top 5 disrupted corridors

After proving product-market fit on Hamburg–Bremen, replicate on Frankfurt–Mannheim (under renovation until 2030), Munich S-Bahn, Ruhrgebiet network, Berlin–Brandenburg ring. Each corridor = new organic growth loop.

Distribution advantages

Viral by nature

"My app told me to stay home today and I dodged a 90-minute delay" is the kind of story people share at the office. Every disrupted morning = free word of mouth.

API partnership potential

Commute intelligence as a service for day-planning apps (Notion Calendar, Morgen, Reclaim.ai), corporate travel tools, and smart home routines. Data moat grows with every user.

05 — Why Us / Why Now

The moment is now

Three forces converging: DB's worst reliability in 21 years, the Deutschlandticket putting millions on transit, and LLMs making real-time reasoning cheap enough to run per-user.

Why now

  • DB punctuality hit historic low (62.5%) — and €17B renovation plan means years of construction disruptions ahead
  • 14.6M Deutschlandticket users = largest wave of new transit riders in German history, all experiencing the pain fresh
  • Gemini Flash / GPT-4o mini make per-user LLM reasoning viable at <€0.01/decision
  • ElevenLabs + Whisper make voice-first interfaces production-ready for the first time
  • 82% of knowledge-economy firms now hybrid — "should I commute?" is a daily question that didn't exist pre-2020

What we built in 48 hours

  • Working mobile app (React Native / Expo) with voice recording and playback
  • Real-time train data pipeline (transport.rest API → n8n → Supabase)
  • AI reasoning engine (Gemini) that produces actionable commute decisions
  • Voice conversation loop: speak → STT → LLM → TTS → audio response
  • Push notification system for proactive morning alerts
  • Full "store once, reason many" architecture for multi-turn context

Why this team

We are commuters on this exact route. We've personally experienced the RE4 cancellation cascade, the "should I just work from home?" dilemma, and the frustration of DB Navigator telling you about a problem when it's already too late. We built this because we needed it yesterday.

The vision

Commute Copilot becomes your daily mobility brain. Starting with "should I commute?", expanding to "how should I plan my whole day around transit reality?" Integrates with calendars, smart homes, employers, and eventually any planning app that needs to know: can this person physically get there on time?
